# Tacklebox — Closed testing kit

Everything to run the Play closed test and clear the **12 testers × 14 days** requirement
for a new individual developer account.

## How the requirement works
- Create a **Closed testing** track in Play Console and upload `tacklebox-1.8.aab`.
- Add testers by email (a Google Group, or an email list). You need **at least 12 testers
  opted in and staying opted in for 14 continuous days**.
- Each tester opens your **opt-in link**, accepts, installs from Google Play, and **keeps the
  app installed** for the fortnight. Ask them not to opt out.
- After 14 days with 12+ testers, Play lets you apply to promote to Production.

> Aim for ~15 testers, not exactly 12 — a couple always drop off or forget to opt in.

2. Install Tacklebox from Google Play (it may take a few minutes to appear).

It's a private on-device app — no account, no ads, no tracking. Thank you!

## C) Tester onboarding — step by step (what they do)
1. Receive the **opt-in URL** from you (Console generates it once testers are added).
2. Open it on their Android phone → **Become a tester**.
3. Wait a few minutes, then install **Tacklebox** from Google Play.
4. Use it a little over the fortnight; keep it installed.
5. Send feedback to you (email / chat).

## D) Suggested 5-minute test checklist (share with testers)
- Onboarding → start with sample waters.
- Log a catch: add a photo, pick a species, enter a weight, save.
- Check it appears as a Personal Best on the Vault.
- Open **Bite windows** — do the times look sensible for today?
- Open **Waters → River conditions** and **Tides & sea** (needs signal) — do they load or fail gracefully?
- Open **Insights** and **Year on the Water**.
- Try **My Tacklebox**: add a gear item and a rig preset.
- Toggle **Settings → units** (metric/imperial) and check weights update.
- Anything crash, look off, or confuse you?
