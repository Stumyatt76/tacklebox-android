# Tacklebox — "What's new" release notes

Goes in the production release's **Release notes** field (per language, e.g. `en-GB`).
Limit: **500 characters** per language.

## First public release  (~360 chars)

```
Welcome to Tacklebox — a calm, private home for your fishing.

• Log catches with photo, species, weight, rig and bait
• Auto-stamped conditions and a personal-best board
• Bite windows (solunar) worked out on your device
• Live weather, tides and river levels when you want them
• Insights and a shareable Year on the Water

Fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking. Tight lines.
```

## Short alternative  (~140 chars)

```
Your private, offline fishing journal. Log catches, track waters and sessions, read bite windows, and see your season come together. No account, no ads.
```

---

## Template for future updates
Keep it human and specific — lead with what the angler gets, not version numbers.

```
What's new in this update:
• <the main improvement, in plain words>
• <a fix or smaller change>
Thanks for the feedback — keep it coming. Tight lines.
```

*Tip: Play shows only the latest "What's new". Rewrite it each release; don't append.*
