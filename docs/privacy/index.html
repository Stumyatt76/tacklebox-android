Tacklebox for Android

Privacy Policy

Draft updated 9 September 2026

In short: Tacklebox is a private, offline-first fishing journal. No Tacklebox account is required. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking. Your catches, photos, waters, sessions and gear are stored only on your device. The app connects to the internet for requested live data, optional iNaturalist connection and Google Play entitlement checks.

Who we are

Tacklebox for Android (“the app”) is developed by Myatt Management Services Limited (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. It applies to the app only.

Our approach

  • No Tacklebox account. Optional iNaturalist sign-in uses that provider’s account.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for marketing.
  • Your journal data lives on your device. Every core feature works fully offline.

Data the app handles

On-device journal data

Your catches, photos, waters, fishing sessions, gear, presets, and settings are stored locally on your device using the app’s database. This data is not transmitted to us or to any server. It stays on your device unless you choose to back it up (see below).

Location

If you enable live conditions, the app requests location permission and uses your approximate location only at that moment to fetch nearby weather, marine, tide, and river information. Your location is used to make those requests and is not stored as a precise fishing spot, and is not shared with us. When a catch records conditions, only the resulting weather summary (for example air temperature or moon phase) is saved on your device — not your coordinates.

Camera and photos

You can attach photos to catches from your camera or photo library. These images are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you use the optional features described below.

Portable backups and PDF exports

Create a photo backup or Season Book PDF locally, then choose its destination through Android’s share sheet. Backups are not encrypted. Protect exported copies and delete them from each destination when no longer needed. There is no direct Google Drive integration.

Optional species identification

Sign in to iNaturalist through your browser. Tacklebox never sees your password. OAuth connection tokens are encrypted using Android Keystore. The legacy manually supplied API token remains in the app’s private database; credentials are excluded from backups. Identification sends the selected photo and applicable approximate location only when requested. Disconnect removes the local connection; revoke provider access in your iNaturalist account.

Purchases and session notifications

Two sessions are free. The optional one-time Unlimited purchase is processed by Google Play. Tacklebox verifies and caches the signed purchase receipt on-device; no card details or receipts are sent to a developer server. Resetting the journal does not reset the allowance. Optional notifications show session time and catch count without water names or photos. A rounded location and timestamp may be kept locally for bite windows.

Third-party services

When you use live conditions or optional features, requests go directly from your device to the relevant provider. Depending on which features you use, these may include:

  • Open-Meteo — weather and marine/tide conditions (receives approximate coordinates).
  • Environment Agency (UK) real-time flood-monitoring — river gauge data (receives approximate coordinates).
  • iNaturalist — only if you use species identification (receives the photo you choose).
  • Google Play — optional purchases and entitlement checks.
  • USGS, NOAA and WorldTides — relevant river and tide information.

These providers handle any data they receive under their own privacy policies. Requests use only the data needed for the feature; optional iNaturalist and Google Play requests use their respective account authorizations.

Permissions

Location — used only to fetch live weather, marine, tide, and river conditions when you request them.

Camera / Photos — used only to attach a photo to a catch, at your choice.

You can grant or deny these at any time in your device settings. Denying them does not affect the app’s offline journaling features.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control of it. You can delete your catches, waters, sessions, gear and presets from within the app (Settings → delete), or remove everything by uninstalling the app. Manage or delete exported backups in each destination you chose.

Children

Tacklebox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
